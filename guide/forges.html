How to identify a forge

The forge is the brown square you may find throughout an area. There are multiple forges scattered around the floor. To interact with them, simply overlap your character to the forge then press your interact button.

How they work

Forges are the way to upgrade your melees. Forges merge two swords and combine them into one sword. When you open the forge menu you will be greeted with two columns full of the swords in your inventory.

The left column is the base sword
The right column is the modifier sword

The base sword is the base sword, the new sword will inherit the attributes and stats of the base sword. The modifier sword will add stats and abilities to the base sword. The special abilities of swords always transfer.

Once two separate swords are selected you can preview the outcome on the right. Forges take materials gained from defeating enemies. If you have the materials you can merge your swords in to one more powerful sword.
