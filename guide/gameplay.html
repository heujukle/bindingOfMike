Gameplay overview

When you start the game you start in a starting room. You can move, switch items and open you inventory. Dashing is also available (double press movement key), it uses stamina but moves you fast and makes you invulnerable for a short time. The starting room also includes test dummies if you want to test your character. If you look top to the top left you will see your some important information
  • Items: default being a projectile and a sword. Swap between them will the scroll wheel
  • Health: the red bar, if it reaches 0 the game ends
  • Stamina: the blue bar, used for dashing
  • money: current amount of money
Once you spawn in you can venture out into other rooms to begin exploring.

Inventory and Stats

The inventory contains all your characters useful information. in the top left you see your current area, health, and stamina. It also shows how much money you have. One important feature is the save game button, it will trigger a file download, and save a JSON file to your computer. This can be loaded on the starting page.
The left side also shows your character stats, these can be improved as a run progresses.
  • speed: how fast the character moves
  • pSpeed: how fast projectiles travel
  • pDamage: projectile damage
  • multishot: how many projectiles you fire
  • maxHealth: max amount of health
  • maxStamina: max amount of stamina
  • dashSpeed: how far you dash + how long you are invulnerable
  • staminaRegen: how fast stamina regenerates
  • shoot speed: cooldown between projectiles
The rest of the inventory is split between three sections:
  • melee: contains swords
  • passive: items that change the way your character plays
  • Materials: materials gained for forging and crafting

Maps

The map is essential to navigating throughout the game. It shows all the rooms you’ve explored. The borders around the rooms indicate no door, while no border indicates you can explore that direction. Bright green highlights the room you are in.
The map has three modes:
  • mini map
  • map overlay (seen on right)
  • no map
It can be cycled through by pressing your map button (m as default)
