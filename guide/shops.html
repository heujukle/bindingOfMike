How to identify a shop

The shop is the yellow square you may find throughout an area. There are multiple shops scattered around the floor. To interact with them, simply overlap your character to the shop then press your interact button.

How they work

The shop is the most important way to make your character stronger, it allows you to buy stats, items, and swords. When you open the shop you will be granted with a variety of options.
Each option has its price displayed beneath it. Money is earned from defeating enemies, and it is spent on items. The players wallet is displayed in the top left, and in the inventory. Once an item is bought from the shop it is instantly placed in the player inventory.
